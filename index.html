Crowns is developed and published by Turnpoint Games (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Crowns (“Crowns” or the “App”) handles information when you use it.

Crowns is a logic puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, payment information, or other profile information in order to play.

1. Information stored on your device

Crowns stores information locally on your device so that the App can function and preserve your progress.

This may include:

  • campaign progress;
  • level results and best results;
  • Standard and Expert difficulty results;
  • application settings;
  • selected language;
  • preferred difficulty;
  • onboarding and Quick Help state;
  • current game session state;
  • generated and custom puzzle state where necessary.

This gameplay and save-state information is not transmitted to our servers as cloud-save data.

Crowns does not operate a cloud-save service or user-account database.

Android system backup functionality may back up eligible local application data according to the settings and behavior of your Android device and Google account.

Using the in-app Reset Progress function resets applicable local game progress. It does not delete telemetry that has previously been transmitted to Firebase.

Uninstalling Crowns normally removes the App's locally stored data, subject to Android backup and restore functionality.

2. Firebase Analytics

Crowns uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to help us improve the game.

Crowns sends a limited set of structured gameplay and application events.

These events may include information such as:

  • whether onboarding or Quick Help was opened or completed;
  • whether a level was started, completed, restarted, exited, or failed;
  • campaign level number;
  • logical puzzle board size;
  • selected difficulty, such as Standard or Expert;
  • use of hints;
  • gameplay duration and limited gameplay counters;
  • use of Generator or Custom modes;
  • selected application language;
  • selected board-view mode;
  • interactions with About actions such as Feedback, Privacy Policy, or Rate Crowns.

These analytics events use predefined values and are intended to measure application usage and game behavior.

Crowns does not send Firebase Analytics:

  • puzzle solutions;
  • board layouts or region geometry;
  • generated puzzle seeds;
  • Crown or X coordinates;
  • complete save files;
  • tap coordinates or drag paths;
  • screen recordings or session replay;
  • feedback message contents;
  • email addresses;
  • free-form user-entered text.

Information collected automatically by Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect technical and usage information, including:

  • an app-instance or installation-related identifier;
  • app version and application information;
  • session and engagement information;
  • device and operating-system information;
  • approximate geographic information.

Crowns does not request Android precise or approximate location permissions and does not send GPS coordinates.

Google Analytics may use the network IP address during data collection to derive approximate geographic information. According to Google's documentation for Google Analytics 4, the IP address is discarded before measurement data is logged in Analytics.

Crowns does not create or assign its own analytics user ID and does not set Firebase custom user properties.

3. Firebase Crashlytics

Crowns uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose application crashes and improve stability.

When Crowns experiences a qualifying native application crash, Crashlytics may automatically collect diagnostic information such as:

  • crash and exception information;
  • stack traces;
  • relevant application state;
  • relevant device and operating-system information;
  • application version information;
  • crash/session identifiers;
  • a Crashlytics installation UUID;
  • Firebase installation and session information used by the Firebase services.

Crowns also reports a limited number of uncaught JavaScript errors, unhandled promise rejections, and critical script-loading failures. These reports contain a generic error category, application build, a high-level screen name, and up to four bundled source filenames and line numbers. Raw error messages, arbitrary rejection values, full URLs, board contents, solutions, and user-entered information are not forwarded by this adapter.

Because Firebase Analytics is also enabled, Analytics events may be available to Crashlytics as breadcrumbs describing application activity before a crash.

Crowns does not manually attach:

  • a Crowns user ID;
  • your name or email address;
  • custom Crashlytics user properties;
  • custom Crashlytics keys;
  • manual Crashlytics logs;
  • complete save data;
  • puzzle solutions;
  • puzzle seeds;
  • board layouts;
  • Crown or X positions;
  • user-entered text.

The limited unhandled JavaScript diagnostics described above are submitted as non-fatal reports.

Handled errors and console output are not generically forwarded to Crashlytics.

4. Firebase installation and session identifiers

Firebase services may generate technical identifiers associated with a particular installation or application session, including a Firebase Installation ID, Crashlytics installation identifiers, and session identifiers.

These identifiers are managed by Firebase for purposes such as analytics measurement, service operation, and crash reporting.

They are not Crowns accounts, and Crowns does not associate them with a name, email address, or developer-created user profile.

5. Advertising

Crowns does not contain advertising in its current version.

Crowns does not integrate Google Mobile Ads, AdMob, or a third-party advertising or marketing attribution SDK.

The App does not request the Android Advertising ID permission and does not request Android Privacy Sandbox advertising ID or attribution permissions.

We do not use Crowns analytics data to operate an in-app advertising system.

6. Location

Crowns does not request access to your device's precise or approximate location through Android location permissions.

Crowns does not collect GPS coordinates.

Firebase Analytics may derive approximate geographic information from network information as described in Google's documentation.

7. Other device data and permissions

Crowns does not request access to:

  • contacts;
  • camera;
  • microphone;
  • photos or media libraries;
  • SMS or call logs;
  • calendar;
  • precise or approximate Android location;
  • Bluetooth or nearby devices;
  • NFC;
  • biometric information;
  • health information.

Crowns does not contain payment processing or in-app purchases in its current version.

8. External services and links

The third-party background data service currently used by Crowns is Google Firebase, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics and their required supporting Firebase components.

Google processes information received through Firebase according to Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Crowns may also contain user-initiated actions that open external applications or pages, such as a privacy-policy page, feedback contact method, or Google Play page.

Opening an external service may cause that service to process information according to its own privacy policy. Crowns does not automatically transmit the contents of a feedback message through its analytics system.

9. Data sharing

Crowns does not sell user data.

Information described in this policy is transmitted to Google/Firebase where necessary to provide the Analytics and Crashlytics services used by Crowns.

We do not integrate third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

The classification of data transfers under applicable privacy laws may depend on the role and terms of the applicable service provider.

10. Data retention and deletion

Local game data is generally stored until it is:

  • reset through applicable in-app controls;
  • removed by clearing application data;
  • removed when the App is uninstalled, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Analytics and crash-reporting information transmitted to Firebase may be retained according to the Firebase/Google service configuration and applicable Google retention practices.

The in-app Reset Progress function affects Crowns local gameplay progress only. It does not request deletion of analytics or crash information previously transmitted to Firebase.

Crowns does not maintain user accounts and therefore does not provide an account-deletion function.

11. Data security

Crowns uses established Firebase SDKs to transmit analytics and crash-reporting information.

Google Analytics documents that measurement data is transmitted to its collection infrastructure using encrypted HTTPS connections.

No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to limit the information collected by Crowns to what is reasonably necessary for application analytics and stability.

12. Children's privacy

Crowns does not require users to provide their age and does not create user profiles.

The App does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other account information in order to play.

Any requirements that apply specifically to distribution of the App to children or particular age groups will be handled according to the applicable store and legal requirements.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide rights relating to personal data, such as rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or complaint to an appropriate supervisory authority.

Crowns does not maintain user accounts or developer-assigned user identifiers. This can affect our ability to associate a particular person with provider-managed Firebase telemetry.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us using the address below.

When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this policy will be updated.
